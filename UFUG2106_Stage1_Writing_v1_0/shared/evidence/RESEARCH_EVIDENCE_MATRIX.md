## What is certified and what is not

- `PASS` and `target_met` were measured with the instructor's evaluator on the released case set; the separate independent verifier also checked supplied output circuits. The verifier exhaustively enumerated smaller cases and sampled larger ones. Do not describe sampled verification as an exhaustive proof.
- Correctness proofs for Boolean identities are universal; *algorithm optimality* and *generalization to unreleased Stage 2* are not established.
- Runtime across archived rounds is not strictly comparable due to run order, environment load and time-based search cutoffs. For causal speed claims use pairs in `paired_benchmarks.csv`.
- Reported 25-case area totals are descriptive; not an official numerical grade.
- Holdout cases generated after algorithm choices can help falsify case-name overfitting, but a synthetic generator is not a guarantee of hidden distribution performance.
- External literature is used for related work and future work, not proof that external algorithms were implemented here.
- **Human contribution audit pending:** actual two-person role attribution and final submission snapshot require student confirmation. Do not invent them.
